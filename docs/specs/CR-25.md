## What
One fun company poll per week, opens Monday, results revealed Friday 3 PM on the Dashboard. Examples: 'Who would survive longest in a zombie apocalypse?', 'Which project deserves its own Netflix documentary?', 'What should the next social activity be?', 'Who is most likely to reply "Noted" within seven seconds?', 'Unijaya's unofficial national food?'

## Rules
Voting anonymous: the vote is stored without identity; a separate hashed participation receipt (user + poll) prevents double voting and cannot be joined back to the choice. Question bank curated by HR/Admin; anyone can suggest. Banned: performance, appearance, personal life, anything that could embarrass. 'Who' questions only from a positive/funny template list and the named person can opt out before publication. Social-activity polls feed CR-18's idea subtask.

## Acceptance
Monday poll appears, Yati votes, cannot vote twice; Friday 3 PM results shown as percentages; no one, including Director, can see who voted what.
