## Requirement
The management meeting is every Friday 5:00 PM. Track sends a reminder email (and in-app notification) every Friday at 3:00 PM to all managers (PM, PE, Sr PM, Director, HR/Finance managers) asking them to complete their Track weekly update for each project they own before the meeting.

## Why
Track AI and the meeting are only as good as what was logged that week ('I don't have access to last week's blockers'). A fixed 2-hour window before the meeting gets the updates in while they are fresh.

## Scope
1. **Schedule:** Friday 3:00 PM Asia/Kuala_Lumpur via the recurring engine (CR-18); if Friday is a public holiday, send Thursday 3:00 PM (meeting assumed moved); HR can pause for company-wide leave.
2. **Recipients:** everyone assigned as PM or PE on an active Track project, plus roles configured as 'management meeting attendee'. One email per person, not per project.
3. **Content** (generic, same for everyone): subject 'Management meeting today 5 PM, update your Track'; body: 'Hello managers, let's prep for the management meeting. Please update your Track entry before 5 PM.' with one 'Open Track' button. No per-project lists, no counts.
3b. **Friday morning T.A.A. task** (system-created): every Friday 8:00 AM the recurring engine (CR-18) creates one individual Task per manager (PM/PE, Sr PM, Director, managers) titled 'Update Track for management meeting', due Friday 5:00 PM, label 'System', project tag 'URSB : Management meeting'. Not a group task, each person is Primary Owner of their own card: when Ahmad finishes he drags his to Done; Nurin's stays open on Nurin's board until she is done. Cards are system-generated (CR-19): closed manually by the owner, excluded from awards and from overdue-award counts, but still visible as overdue on the CR-17 panel after 5 PM so the Director sees who hasn't updated.
4. **Escalation:** none beyond the email; the open T.A.A. cards after 5 PM are the signal.
5. **Meeting pack:** at 4:55 PM Track generates the meeting view (all projects, this week's updates, blockers, money out) so the meeting reads from Track, not slides. Track AI is pointed at the same data so 'What were last week's blockers?' works.
6. Reminder times, recipients and meeting day editable by Director/Admin; every send logged.
