## What
Optional short challenges with nothing to do with KPI: 'Have lunch with someone outside your project', 'Share one useful AI prompt', 'Teach a colleague one shortcut', 'Post your workstation view', 'Recommend one book, show or cafe', 'Ask one person what they're building'. Completing one gives a temporary fun badge (30 days) on the profile, no points.

## Rules
2 to 3 quests live at a time in The Playground; completion is self-declared with a photo or one-liner posted to a Side Quest feed; others can react. Badges expire, never count anywhere. HR/Admin curate the quest list; staff can suggest.
