## What
Every Friday from 3 PM, two one-tap prompts on the Dashboard: This week was: Productive / Chaotic / Suspiciously Peaceful / I Survived, and My win this week: ______ (optional, 1 line). Company mood shown anonymously Friday 5 PM: '46% Productive, 31% Chaotic, 23% Refusing to Elaborate.'

## Rules
Mood answers stored without identity; a hashed participation receipt prevents double submission and cannot be linked to the answer. Aggregate shown only with minimum 5 responses. Individual mood responses never appear on management dashboards or reports, and cannot be exported. 'My win' is posted under the user's name only if they tick 'share'; otherwise private. Closes Monday 9 AM.

## Acceptance
Friday 3 PM prompt appears; tap once, done; 5 PM company mood shows percentages; Director view has no per-person mood anywhere; with 4 responses, mood is hidden.
