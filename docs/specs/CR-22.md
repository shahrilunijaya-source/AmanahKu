## What
On the 1st working day of each month, a swipeable, shareable story-card recap for each person, Spotify Wrapped style. Cards such as: 'You closed 27 cards', 'You survived 8 High-priority situations', 'Most productive day: Tuesday', 'You helped 6 different people', 'September character arc: Somehow Got It Done'. A company version on the Dashboard: '286 cards closed, 19 lessons shared, 7 fires extinguished and only 43 "urgent" tasks.'

## Rules
Personal Wrapped is private by default; user chooses to share to the Wall. No analysis of comment text or phrases, numbers and dates only. Character-arc titles from a curated list (HR editable, 30+ entries) selected by simple rules. Uses the CR-14 frozen snapshot so numbers match awards. No comparison to other people.

## Acceptance
1 Oct, Yati sees her September Wrapped (6 to 8 cards) and can share it; Dashboard shows company Wrapped with reactions; a user with 'Keep it plain' gets a plain text summary.
