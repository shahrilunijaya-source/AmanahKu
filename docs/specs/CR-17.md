## Requirement
For roles Director, HR and Senior Manager (Sr PM and above), the Dashboard must highlight, at the top and prominently: (a) attendance lateness across staff, and (b) all pending T.A.A. tasks that are past due, company-wide. Other roles keep the normal personal dashboard.

## Why
Management currently has to open Attendance and each staff's T.A.A. board to find problems. Exceptions should come to them on the first screen, every day.

## Scope
**A. Lateness panel** – today: who is late / not clocked in yet (with minutes late); this month: late shifts per person (count and trend), absent and incomplete shifts. Sortable, click name to attendance detail. Threshold colouring (e.g. 3+ late = red).

**B. Overdue tasks panel** – every T.A.A. card (Task / Assignment / Adhoc / subtask) past due and not Done, grouped by assignee, showing days overdue, priority, project and last activity. Filters: project, person, priority, days overdue. Totals by person and by project. Includes tagged/subtask items (CR-04/05).

**C. Placement** – both panels above 'Current month summary' for these roles; collapsible; 'My staff' toggle scoped to their team for Sr Managers, whole company for Director/HR.

**D. Actions** – from the panel: Nudge (reminder to Primary Owner; max 1 per card per day; logged), Reassign (only the person's line manager, PM of that project, or Director; reason required; original owner keeps the overdue record per CR-14 rule 8; owner and new owner notified), open card. Every nudge/reassign written to the audit log (who, when, card, from to, reason). Daily 8:00 AM digest email/notification to these roles with the two counts.

**E. Access** – role-based; staff cannot see other staff's lateness. HR sees lateness company-wide but cannot reassign tasks; Sr Managers see and act only within their reporting line.
