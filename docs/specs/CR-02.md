## Why
As Sr Project Manager, Yati needs to review all staff timesheets for project manday tracking and reporting, same as the existing 'See all staff tasks' access on the T.A.A. board.

## Scope
Grant view access to all staff timesheets for Yati's account/role. Filters by staff, project and date range should work as for admin users. Read-only is sufficient unless approval rights are also required (to be confirmed).

## Acceptance
1. Logged in as Yati, click 'See all staff timesheets', full staff timesheet list loads without permission error.
2. Filters and export (if available) work the same as for admin.
