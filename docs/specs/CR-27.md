## What
One unexpected award per month, category unknown until reveal, added as the last slide in the CR-14 carousel. Examples: Human Google, Calm in the Chaos, Client Translator, Bug Whisperer, PowerPoint Has Left the Chat, The 'I Already Fixed It' Award, Professional Tab Collector, Most Likely to Save the Day Quietly, Meeting Survivor of the Month.

## Rules
Manually selected by Director or a rotating 3-person 'mystery committee', who write a funny but kind explanation. Not counted toward Hall of Fame or any streak. Same person can't win two months in a row. Logged in audit like other manual awards, but no rubric needed, it is meant to be a surprise.

## Acceptance
Mystery Award slide reveals on the 1st with category, winner and explanation; category not visible anywhere before publish.
