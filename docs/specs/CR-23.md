## What
Every person gets 3 Flowers per month to give to colleagues with a short note (required, max 200 chars), e.g. 'For fixing the production issue before anyone started panicking.' Appears on the recipient's profile Wall and in a small Dashboard feed. No nomination, no approval.

## Rules
Unused Flowers expire monthly (no banking). Cannot give to yourself; max 1 Flower per recipient per month from the same giver. Giver name shown (not anonymous). Flowers are not an award input and never feed CR-14 or management views. HR can hide a note that breaks conduct rules (logged).
