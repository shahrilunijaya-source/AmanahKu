## What
When an important card or milestone moves to Done, the team can ring the bell: 'WE HAVE MOVEMENT: MySToDS Release 4 is officially Done.' Confetti, team avatars, reactions on the Dashboard.

## Rules
Only cards flagged Milestone by PM and above (or Track WBS milestones) can trigger; ordinary cards cannot, so it stays rare. The Primary Owner or PM rings it manually after Done (one click, optional line). Max 3 bells per project per month. Shown for 24 hours, then in Wins page. Respects 'Keep it plain'.

## Acceptance
Move a Milestone card to Done, 'Ring the bell?' prompt; ring it, Dashboard celebration with team avatars; a non-milestone card shows no prompt.
