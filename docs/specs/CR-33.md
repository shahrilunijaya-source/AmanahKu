## Requirement
Replace the fixed 'Good morning, Yati.' with a rotating, context-aware greeting drawn from a curated bank (EN + BM, HR/Admin editable, 60+ lines). One line per page load, never the same line twice in a row, personalised with first name. Cheeky but kind; never about performance or lateness.

## Context buckets
**Time of day:** early (before 8) 'Sunrise squad reporting in, Yati.' / morning 'Morning, Yati. Coffee first, then the world.' / after lunch 'Afternoon, Yati. The post-lunch fog is temporary.' / evening 'Evening, Yati. Wrap it up like a pro.'

**Day of week:** Monday 'Monday, Yati. We've survived worse.' / Wednesday 'Halfway there, Yati.' / Friday 'Friday, Yati. The finish line has Wi-Fi.' / Saturday 'Weekend warrior mode, Yati?'

**Situation:** first login of the month 'New month, new cards, same legend, hi Yati.' / all cards clear 'Nothing overdue, Yati. Suspicious, but well done.' / rainy day (Kota Bharu weather) 'Hujan di luar, Yati. Sempurna untuk kerja dalam.' / long weekend ahead 'Hi Yati. Long weekend loading...'

**Personal:** birthday 'It's your day, Yati.' (with CR-13) / work anniversary 'Three years of Yati at Unijaya. Legend.' / back from leave 'Welcome back, Yati. We kept your seat warm.'

**BM examples:** 'Selamat pagi, Yati. Jom, satu kad demi satu.' / 'Petang yang produktif, Yati?' / 'Jumaat, Yati. Sikit lagi.'

## Rules
Priority order: Personal, then Situation, then Day, then Time; one line only. Falls back to a plain 'Good morning, Yati.' under 'Keep it plain' (CR-31). Never references overdue counts, lateness, leave balance or anyone else. Staff can suggest lines; HR approves into the bank. Weather lines only if a weather source is configured, otherwise skipped.

## Acceptance
1. Ten page loads on a Tuesday morning, at least 3 different morning/Tuesday lines, no immediate repeats.
2. Friday 4 PM, a Friday or evening line.
3. Birthday, birthday line wins over all others.
4. 'Keep it plain', 'Good morning, Yati.' only.
5. Toggle BM, BM line shown.
