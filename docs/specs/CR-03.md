## Requirement
Timesheet must be submittable per day, not only as a full week. Currently 'Submit week' is blocked until every weekday has a percentage (e.g. 'Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday has a line with no percentage yet').

## Why
Staff fill timesheets daily but cannot submit until Friday; incomplete earlier days block the whole week. Daily submission keeps records current and lets managers see progress mid-week.

## Scope
1. Submit day action per day alongside Save draft; 'Submit week' remains as a shortcut that submits all remaining days with per-day validation.
2. Deadline: each day must be submitted by 10:00 AM the next working day (configurable); after that the day is 'Late submission' (visible to manager, affects Timesheet? Already Done.).
3. Lock: a submitted day is read-only for the staff. Corrections only via manager Return for Correction (reason required), day reopens, staff edits, resubmits. Every change logged: who, when, old value to new value, reason.
4. Manager approval: per-day status: Draft, Submitted, Approved / Returned. Manager can bulk-approve a week. Approved days are locked for everyone except HR (audit reason required).
5. Rejection: Returned days show the manager's comment and a resubmit button; resubmission resets the deadline clock only for that day and is flagged 'Resubmitted'.
6. Leave, weekends, public holidays: auto-filled from Leave and the holiday calendar as locked non-editable rows (lock icon); no submission required and they never block Submit week. Approved half-day leave pre-fills the remaining hours as required.
7. Zero-hour days: a working day with no lines needs an explicit reason (e.g. no allocation, training, offsite) before it can be submitted; cannot be left blank.
8. Backdated entries: staff may submit up to 3 working days back; beyond that requires manager unlock (reason logged). HR can set a monthly cut-off after which nothing can change without HR.
9. Weekly view shows per-day status badges; Dashboard 'Timesheet %' = approved days divided by working days to date.

## Acceptance
1. On Friday, submit Fri only, success even if Mon to Thu are incomplete.
2. Submitted day is locked; staff cannot edit; manager 'Return for Correction' reopens it with reason, staff resubmits, change log shows old to new.
3. 'Submit week' submits all not-yet-submitted days with per-day validation; leave and public holiday rows are skipped.
4. Submitting Monday after Tuesday 10:00 AM marks it 'Late submission'.
5. A working day with no lines cannot be submitted without a reason.
6. Staff cannot edit a day older than 3 working days without manager unlock.
7. Dashboard Timesheet % counts approved days only.
