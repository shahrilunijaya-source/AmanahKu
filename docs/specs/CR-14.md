## Requirement
An Awards segment on the Dashboard (prominent, same banner style as CR-13) announcing the month's award winners on the 1st working day of each month. Most awards are auto-computed from Amanahku data; a few are nominated/manual. Everyone can react (emoji) and comment on each award.

## Why
Recognise consistency and contribution using data the system already collects, so appreciation is fair, visible and effortless.

## Rules (kept deliberately light)
1. Computed on the 1st working day for the previous month from the frozen snapshot (Global Clause); ties, both win.
2. One Primary Owner per card, only the Primary Owner gets completion credit; helpers get helper credit only.
3. One completion counted per card, first move to Done only; reopen/redo adds nothing.
4. System-generated cards do not count (events, calendar, recurring, award tasks, CR-19).
5. Approved leave and non-working days are neutral for all attendance awards.
6. Overdue? Couldn't Be Me requires at least 5 assigned cards in the month.
7. Chief Hype Officer counts unique colleagues, not reaction quantity.
8. The Question Department counts distinct sessions participated in.
9. One person can win a maximum of two awards per month; extra wins pass to the runner-up.
10. Same person cannot win the same award two months in a row; 3 wins of any award gives a Hall of Fame badge.
11. Nominations open the last week of the month; awards and reactions archived on the winner's profile Wall (CR-13).

That is the full list. No manday weighting, no approval per card, no rubrics, no duplicate reviews. If someone games it, the Mystery Award committee can gently roast them.

## Manual awards entry
Awards screen under The Playground (CR-16) with tabs: This month's winners / Nominate / Past winners.

**Nominate tab** (all staff, last week of month): pick award (Main Character Energy / Office Yoda), pick colleague, one-line reason. One nomination per award per person; cannot nominate yourself; most votes wins.

**Select tab** (PM and above / Director): see nomination results; pick 'New but Dangerous'; Director keys in 'The Chosen One' with a one-line reason. Reasons appear on the carousel slide.

**Auto-task to T.A.A.:** on the last Monday of each month, a recurring task (CR-18 engine) 'Nominate this month's awards' is created for all staff (due last working day) and 'Select manual award winners' for PM and above and Director (due 1st working day of next month). Task links straight to the Awards screen and auto-closes when submitted. Auto awards publish together with manual ones once selection is done.

## Dashboard card
Carousel, one award per slide, full-width, same prominent style as CR-13.

Each slide: award icon/illustration, award name with its explanation line underneath, winner photo + name + role, the actual number behind it (e.g. 'Late? Never Heard of Her, 22/22 on-time shifts'), emoji reactions and comment count.

Behaviour: auto-rotates every 6s or so, pauses on hover, swipe/arrow navigation, dot indicators, 'View all' opens the full awards page. Order: manual awards (The Chosen One, Main Character Energy) first, then the rest.

Reactions/comments happen on the slide without leaving the dashboard. Notification to all staff when published. 'Past winners' link to a monthly leaderboard.

## Acceptance
1. On 1 Oct, awards for September are published automatically with correct winners from the frozen snapshot.
2. Carousel shows one award per slide, auto-rotates and can be swiped; staff can react and comment on the slide.
3. A person who won CEO of Beating the Traffic in Sept cannot win it in Oct.
4. Awards screen appears in The Playground; Nominate and Select tasks auto-create and auto-close on submission.
5. Winner's profile shows the award badge.
6. Card with Primary Owner Emysha, helper Adri, Emysha gets completion credit, Adri gets helper credit only.
7. Move a card Done, Reopen, Done, counted once.
8. Event cards and recurring tasks never appear in any award count.
9. Someone with 3 assigned cards and zero overdue, not eligible for Overdue? Couldn't Be Me.
10. A person leading in 3 awards wins 2, third goes to runner-up.

## Notes
Weekend work is deliberately not auto-awarded (burnout risk), recognised via 'Main Character Energy' nomination only. Confirm with Director whether any award carries a tangible reward.
