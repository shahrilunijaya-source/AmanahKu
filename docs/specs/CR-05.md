## Requirement
Each card's Subtasks section must support an assignee and due date per subtask (currently text only). A subtask assigned to someone must appear as a task on that person's own T.A.A. board. When all subtasks are completed, the parent card automatically moves to In Review (not Done) and notifies the Reviewer/Primary Owner. A parent card cannot be dragged to Done while any subtask is still open.

## Why
Large assignments (e.g. Prototype POC, TU Gitlab) are split across several people. Today subtasks are invisible to the assignee's board, and cards get moved to Done while pieces are still outstanding.

## Scope
1. Subtask fields: title, Assigned (one Primary Owner, mandatory), Tagged helpers (multi), due date, status (open/done). Roles as defined in CR-04.
2. Assigned subtask appears on the assignee's T.A.A. board as a Task card, labelled 'Subtask of <parent>', linked back to the parent. Counted in their open/overdue totals (see CR-04).
3. Parent card shows subtask progress (e.g. 2/5) and inherits the earliest overdue subtask as an overdue indicator. Optional parent checklist 'Definition of Done' (e.g. documentation, client acceptance) must be ticked before Done.
4. Rule: when all subtasks are completed, the parent moves to In Review automatically (activity log entry, Reviewer/Primary Owner notified). If a Reviewer is assigned, only the Reviewer may move the parent from In Review to Done; the Primary Owner cannot bypass. If no Reviewer is assigned, the Primary Owner may mark it Done. No auto-Done option. Deleting the last open subtask does not trigger the move to In Review or complete the parent; only completion does.
5. Done-guard: dragging a parent to Done with open subtasks is blocked, with a message listing the open subtasks. Archiving follows the same rule.

## Notes
