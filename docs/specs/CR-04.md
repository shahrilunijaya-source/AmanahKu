## Requirement
When a manager opens a staff member's T.A.A. board (e.g. Emysha), it must show both items the staff keyed in themselves and items where they are tagged/assigned by others. Tagged items must carry a visible Tagged label on the card.

## Why
Currently the staff view shows only self-created items (e.g. Emysha shows 0 open / 0 overdue even though she is tagged on other cards). This under-reports workload and hides overdue items when reviewing staff.

## Roles on a card
Four distinct roles, all shown on the card and all filterable:

- **Assigned (Primary Owner)** – exactly one person, accountable for delivery; gets completion credit and overdue responsibility.
- **Tagged** – informed or helping; sub-types Helper (does part of the work, gets collaboration credit) and FYI (view only, no credit). The tagger chooses Helper or FYI when tagging.
- **Creator** – created the record; no credit unless also Assigned.
- **Reviewer** – optional, set by PM; cannot be the same person as Assigned. When set, only the Reviewer may move In Review to Done (Primary Owner cannot bypass).

## Scope
Staff detail board query = Assigned = staff OR Tagged contains staff OR Reviewer = staff. Card labels: 'Assigned' (default, no label), 'Tagged – Helper', 'Tagged – FYI', 'Reviewer'. Counters (open / overdue / blocked / in review) count Assigned items only; Tagged and Reviewer items shown separately ('helping on 3 / reviewing 2'). Filter chips: Assigned / Tagged / Reviewing / All. Apply the same to 'See all staff tasks'.

## Acceptance
1. Tag Emysha as Helper on a card owned by Adri, it appears on her board with 'Tagged – Helper'; her Assigned counters do not change, 'helping on' shows 1.
2. Tag her as FYI, shown with 'Tagged – FYI', no credit, no counter.
3. Set Yati as Reviewer, card appears under her 'Reviewing' filter; only she can move it In Review to Done.
4. Filter 'Assigned' hides tagged and reviewing items.
