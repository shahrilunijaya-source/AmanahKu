## Requirement
When it is a staff member's birthday, the Dashboard shows a prominent birthday card (full-width banner above 'Current month summary', not a small widget) with a company wish, confetti animation on open, and a space where everyone can post their own wish. The birthday person sees the wishes on their own dashboard.

## Why
Celebrate people. The dashboard is the first screen everyone opens daily; a visible, shared celebration builds team culture at zero cost.

## Scope
1. Source: date of birth from staff profile (month/day only, year hidden). Show for the whole day; if birthday falls on a weekend/holiday, show on the next working day too (to confirm).
2. Banner: photo/avatar, name, role, company message ('Selamat Hari Lahir, <name>! from all of us at Unijaya'), confetti plays once per user per day on first open (with reduce-motion respect and a dismiss button).
3. Wishes: inline composer (text + emoji, optional GIF/photo), wishes listed with avatars; reactions allowed. Birthday person's own dashboard shows the banner plus all wishes with a thank-you reply option.
4. Multiple birthdays same day: carousel or stacked cards. Upcoming birthdays (next 7 days) shown as a small line under the banner for others to prepare.
5. Notification to all staff at 8:00 AM ('Today is <name>'s birthday, send a wish'). Wishes archived on the person's profile ('Wall').
6. Staff can opt out of public birthday display in their profile settings.

## Acceptance
1. Set a test staff DOB = today, all staff dashboards show the full-width banner with confetti on first open.
2. Yati posts a wish, appears on the banner for everyone and on the birthday person's dashboard.
3. Two staff with same birthday, both shown.
4. Opt-out staff, no banner shown.
5. Next day, banner gone; wishes visible on the person's profile Wall.
