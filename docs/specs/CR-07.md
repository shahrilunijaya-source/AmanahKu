## Requirement
Make every section of the 'Last Week' card collapsible (What was planned / What happened currently cannot collapse). Reorder sections and change data sources so the card is auto-populated rather than manually typed.

## New order
1. WBS Tasks (from baseline), shown first.
2. What was planned, shown per day (Mon to Sun), listing the T.A.A. cards/subtasks due or scheduled that day, not a single free-text line.
3. What happened, pulled automatically from T.A.A. (cards created, moved, completed, and timesheet lines logged that day). No manual typing.
4. Calendar events, pulled from T.A.A., which in turn syncs from Google Calendar (depends on CR-01).
5. Issues, Money Out, unchanged, remain collapsible.

## Scope
Each section header toggles collapse/expand; state remembered per user. Planned vs Happened aligned side-by-side or stacked per day so gaps are obvious. Items link back to the T.A.A. card. Manual notes still allowed as an extra line per day if needed.

## Acceptance
1. All six sections can collapse and expand individually.
2. WBS Tasks appears at the top.
3. Planned shows one row per day with T.A.A. items due that day.
4. Completing a card in T.A.A. on Tue makes it appear under Tue in 'What happened' without manual entry.
5. A Google Calendar meeting on Wed appears under Calendar events for Wed.

## Dependencies
CR-01 (Google Calendar sync), CR-04 (tagged items), CR-05 (subtasks), so planned/actual lists are complete.
