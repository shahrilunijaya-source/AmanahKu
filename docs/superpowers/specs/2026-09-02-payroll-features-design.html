Design spec · AmanahKu · 2 September 2026

Payroll for Unijaya

What AmanahKu still needs so Unijaya can run its own monthly payroll, pay staff on time, and file EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB and HRD Corp figures that match to the sen, without leaving Worksy's vocabulary behind.

Built shipped on dev, tested Partial exists, needs work Gap to build Law statutory basis

1. Summary

Unijaya runs payroll on Worksy today. AmanahKu already has the hard part: statutory calculators built from the official KWSP, PERKESO and LHDN documents, a Payroll Item catalogue, Fixed and Individual Transactions, payroll runs, payslip PDFs, Form EA, Form E and C.P.8D. One finalized test run exists for August 2026.

What is missing is everything around the calculation that a real month needs: the employer's own statutory numbers, the upload files each agency accepts, HRD Corp levy, proration of basic pay for joiners and leavers, the seven-day payment deadline, monthly TP1 relief claims, the CP22 family of notices, and a payslip that carries every particular the Employment Regulations list. This spec defines those as sixteen features across three phases. Phase 1 is the set that must exist before the first live run.

2. Scope and assumptions

In scope

  • Monthly salary for employees on a contract of service, paid in ringgit into a Malaysian bank account.
  • Malaysian citizens under 60. The EPF and PERKESO calculators already handle 60 and over and non-citizens; nothing in this spec removes that, but no new work targets them.
  • Statutory upload files for KWSP i-Akaun, PERKESO ASSIST, LHDN e-PCB Plus and HRD Corp e-TRiS. The app produces the file; a person uploads it and pays through the agency portal or bank.
  • Annual Form EA, Form E and C.P.8D (already built) fed correctly by the new monthly data.

Out of scope

  • Direct API submission to any agency. None offers a public API a small employer can use; file upload is the sanctioned path.
  • PCB categories for C-suite, knowledge workers and returning experts (LHDN spec D.b.3 to D.b.5). Unijaya has none.
  • Accounting ledger. A journal CSV for the accountant is Phase 3; the app is not a general ledger.
  • Termination and lay-off benefits computation. Unijaya staff over RM4,000 fall outside s.60J anyway; below that it is a rare, HR-computed Individual Transaction.
  • Gig Workers Act 2025. Platform work only, no employees affected.

Assumptions made in this spec

  • Unijaya must register with HRD Corp. 29 Malaysian employees is above the 10-employee mandatory line, and coverage has extended to all sectors since 2021. If Unijaya already pays the levy through Worksy, the registration number exists and only needs entering.
  • Interns are employees for payroll. They draw an allowance through payroll, so EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB apply as for any employee. If any intern is paid outside payroll, they are simply given no salary structure.
  • Worksy's monthly Individual Transaction wording stays. Terms in this spec follow Worksy: Payroll Item, Fixed Transaction, Individual Transaction. Staff cross-check figures against Worksy during the parallel run.
  • Statutory rates stay read-only. Nothing here adds a tenant-editable rate. Only per-person facts (reliefs, opt-ins, notices) are editable.
  • Parallel run. Unijaya runs one month in both systems before switching. Phase 1 is sized for that month.

5. Features to build

Each feature says why the law needs it, what changes, the rules that decide behaviour, and what test proves it. Phase order is delivery order.

PHASE 1

Before the first live run

Without these, the first month cannot be filed. Everything here is small; the point is completeness, not size.

F1 Employer statutory identity Gap

Law Every upload file and annual form is keyed on the employer's own registration numbers.

What changes

  • New tenant fields: epf_employer_no, socso_employer_code, hrdf_registration_no, employer_category (Form E item 3), employer_status (item 4), paying_bank_code, paying_bank_account_no, payroll_contact_name, payroll_contact_phone. employer_tin (the E number) and registration_number (SSM) already exist.
  • Company settings screen gets a "Statutory registration" section for these, editable by HR and management. Super-admin can also edit (existing tenant edit).
  • Form E fills items 3, 4, 5 from the new fields instead of leaving them blank.

Rules

  • Run creation (F2 readiness gate) refuses when EPF number, SOCSO code or E number is blank. HRD Corp number is required only when hrdf_registered is on (F7).
  • Numbers are stored as typed but trimmed; formats are validated loosely (digits and hyphens) since agencies change formats and a hard regex would block real numbers.

Tests

  • Feature test: saving the settings persists them; run creation with a blank EPF number returns 422 naming the missing field; Form E data carries items 3 to 5.

F2 Employee statutory profile and run readiness gate Gap

Law CP39 needs the TIN or NRIC; Borang 8A needs the SOCSO number and NRIC; KWSP Form A needs the EPF number and NRIC. A payslip with a blank identifier is a rejected upload on the 15th.

What changes

  • Move tax_no and nationality conceptually to "who the person is", the same way NRIC and marital status were moved in August. Keep the columns on salary_structures for now to avoid another reconcile migration; the readiness gate reads them wherever they live.
  • Add employees.last_working_day (date, nullable). Today the only leaving signal is an offboarding case; a person archived by hand has none. Offboarding sets it automatically; HR can set it directly.
  • Bank name becomes a picker over a fixed list of Malaysian bank codes (F14 shares it). Existing free text is mapped once by a migration: "Maybank", "MBB", "Malayan Banking Berhad (MAYBANK) (MBB)" all become MBBEMYKL.
  • New "Readiness" panel on the Payroll screen, above "Create run": a list of every active employee with a red mark per missing item: salary structure, basic pay above zero, NRIC, EPF number, SOCSO number, TIN, bank code and account, date of birth, joined date.

Rules

  • Create run is disabled while any active employee is not ready, with the count shown on the button. HR can exclude an employee from the run explicitly (an intern paid outside payroll, say), which records the exclusion on the run.
  • A missing TIN is a warning, not a block: LHDN accepts NRIC in CP39 for staff who have no TIN yet. Every other item blocks.

Tests

  • Feature test: a tenant with one unready employee gets 422 on create run listing the employee and the field; excluding the employee lets the run through and stores the exclusion; a missing TIN alone does not block.

F3 Basic pay proration for joiners and leavers Gap

Law EA s.18A: wages for an incomplete month are monthly wages ÷ days in that month × days employed. Calendar days, the real month length.

What changes

  • Reuse the existing prorationFactor() that Fixed Transactions already use. Apply it to basic salary and to every recurring allowance in salary_structures.allowances when the factor is below 1.
  • Payslip stores days_employed and days_in_month so the payslip PDF can print "23 / 30 days" and so an audit can re-derive the figure.
  • Leaving date comes from employees.last_working_day (F2) first, then the offboarding case as today.

Rules

  • Proration applies only to earnings flagged prorate_on_incomplete_month on the Payroll Item. Basic Salary and Fixed Allowance default on; Bonus, Commission, Overtime and reimbursements default off.
  • Unpaid leave stays on the 26-day ordinary rate. Two divisors coexist by design (section 7).
  • HR can override the prorated basic on the payslip, and the override is flagged the same way overtime overrides are today.

Tests

  • Unit: joiner on 8 February 2028 (29-day month) with RM3,000 basic gets RM2,275.86; leaver on 15 April with RM3,000 gets RM1,500.00; full-month employee is untouched. Feature: the payslip stores the day counts and the override flag.

F4 Wage floor and deduction guards Gap

Law Minimum Wages Order 2024 (RM1,700 from 1 Feb 2025). EA s.24: total deductions in a month may not exceed 50% of wages, and non-statutory deductions need written consent. Limitation of Overtime Regulations 1980: 104 overtime hours a month.

What changes

  • Constant MinimumWage::MONTHLY = 1700.00 with its effective date, in code, next to the other statutory constants. Not tenant-editable.
  • Salary structure save warns (does not block) when basic pay for a full-time employee is below RM1,700. Interns and part-timers have no floor in the Order the same way, so the warning applies to every structure but says "check the employment type".
  • Payslip computation flags deduction_cap_exceeded when non-statutory deductions (loans, advances, zakat, other) exceed 50% of gross wages. The run's review list shows the flag; finalize is blocked while any payslip carries it unless HR ticks "employee consented in writing, DG-permitted where needed" on that payslip.
  • Overtime pull warns when an employee's pulled hours exceed 104 in the period.
  • Fixed Transactions of type deduction gain a consent_reference text field (date of the signed authorisation, or the loan agreement reference).

Tests

  • Unit: cap flag trips at 50.01% and not at 50.00%; statutory deductions never count toward the cap. Feature: finalize returns 422 with the flag set and no consent; passes with consent recorded.

F5 Pay date and the seven-day rule Gap

Law EA s.19: wages no later than the seventh day after the wage period ends. Employment Regulations reg. 5(c): the pay statement shows the date of payment.

What changes

  • payroll_runs.pay_date (date, required at finalize, defaults to the last calendar day of the period) and payroll_runs.paid_at (set when HR clicks "Mark paid" after the bank transfer clears).
  • The payslip PDF prints the pay date (F13).
  • Dashboard card for HR: "August 2026 payroll: finalized 28 Aug, pay by 7 Sep, not yet marked paid." Turns red on the eighth day.

Rules

  • Finalize refuses a pay date later than the seventh day after period end, with the message quoting s.19. HR can override with a reason, which is written to the audit log; the law allows later payment only for overtime and holiday pay, so the override is expected to be rare.

Tests

  • Feature: finalize with pay date 8 September for the August period returns 422; 7 September passes; the override stores the reason and an audit entry.

F6 Statutory upload files: KWSP Form A, PERKESO Borang 8A, LHDN CP39 Gap

Law Each agency accepts one upload format. The CP39 layout is Exhibit 4 of the 2026 LHDN spec (fixed-width text, header 57 characters, detail 136). KWSP i-Akaun accepts its Form A CSV. PERKESO ASSIST accepts a Borang 8A text file. All three are due by the 15th of the month after the wage month.

What changes

  • Three new exporters beside the bank files, one class each, under App\Services\Payroll\Statutory\: KwspFormA, PerkesoBorang8A, LhdnCp39. Each takes a finalized run and returns a string; the controller streams it with the agency's expected file name (CP39: <employerNo>MM_YYYY.txt).
  • Download links on the finalized run: "EPF file", "SOCSO/EIS file", "PCB file", "HRD Corp file" (F7). The existing generic statutory CSV stays as the human-readable check sheet.
  • The official layout documents for Form A and Borang 8A are downloaded into docs/statutory/ alongside the schedules, and each exporter has a golden-file test transcribed from the document's own example. CP39's layout is already in the spec PDF in the repo.

Rules

  • Only finalized runs export. A draft or approved run shows the links disabled with the reason.
  • CP39 header totals must equal the sum of the detail rows; the exporter asserts this and refuses to produce a file that does not balance.
  • Amounts in CP39 are the payslip's pcb + pcb_additional as MTD and cp38 as CP38, each in ringgit with two decimals, zero-padded to width. Names are upper-cased ASCII, truncated at 60. Employees with no TIN carry the NRIC in the IC field and zeros in the TIN field.
  • Borang 8A carries the employee's SOCSO number (NRIC for citizens), the wage subject to contribution (capped at RM6,000), and both shares. SKBBK is reported the way PERKESO's 2026 layout specifies; if the layout does not yet carry it, the exporter emits the pre-SKBBK layout and the SKBBK amount is paid through the portal manually, and this is written into the download page.
  • Form A carries the EPF number, NRIC, name, wages, employee share and employer share per employee, plus the employer's number and the contribution month (wage month plus one).
  • Every download writes an audit entry, as the existing statutory CSV already does, because the file contains NRICs.

Tests

  • Golden files: one two-employee run per exporter, byte-compared against a fixture transcribed from the official layout example. Header-balance assertion. Zero-TIN employee case. Width and padding of every CP39 field checked with a unit test that slices the string at the documented offsets.

F7 HRD Corp levy Gap

Law PSMB Act 2001: employers with 10 or more Malaysian employees register and pay 1% of wages monthly (5 to 9 employees: optional, 0.5%). Wages here are basic salary plus fixed allowances, after unpaid leave, excluding bonus, commission, overtime, travel and shift allowances. Due by the 15th via e-TRiS. It is the employer's cost; nothing is deducted from the employee.

What changes

  • Tenant setting hrdf_registered (off, 1%, 0.5%) beside the registration number from F1. Registry entry in Features::SETTINGS, tenant scope, default off.
  • Payroll Item gains hrdf_liable. Seeded: Basic Salary and Fixed Allowance on, everything else off. Unpaid Leave Deduction reduces the base the same way it reduces the EPF base today.
  • Payslip gains hrdf_levy (employer side) and it is added to employer_cost. It never appears in the employee's deductions.
  • Levy applies to citizens only: the salary structure's nationality must be citizen, otherwise zero.
  • Exporter HrdCorpLevyFile in F6's folder produces the e-TRiS wages-and-levy listing; the run page shows the month's total.

Rules

  • Levy is rounded to the sen, half up, per employee, then summed. If HRD Corp's guideline rounds at the total instead, the test fixture from their example decides; this is verified when the layout document is fetched.
  • Readiness (F2) warns when the setting is off and the tenant has 10 or more active citizen employees.

Tests

  • Unit: RM3,000 basic + RM300 fixed allowance + RM500 overtime + 2 unpaid days (26-day rate) yields levy on RM3,069.23, that is RM30.69. Non-citizen yields zero. Setting off yields zero and no column on the payslip PDF.
PHASE 2

Monthly operations

Needed within the first quarter of live use. Each one turns something HR would otherwise track in a spreadsheet into a record the calculators read.

F8 TP1 monthly claims and the RM6,000 travel exemption Gap

Law LHDN spec 2026: an employee may hand in Form TP1 each month claiming reliefs (medical, education, insurance, SOCSO/EIS RM350, and the 2026 additions) and zakat paid outside payroll. These enter the year-to-date ∑LP and Z variables. Petrol, travel and toll allowances are tax-exempt up to RM6,000 a year and reported in EA Part F.

What changes

  • New table payroll_tp1_claims: employee, year, month, relief code (from the TP1(1/2026) list, stored as a PHP constant with the yearly caps), amount, zakat amount, note, created by. HR enters it from the paper or PDF form the employee signs; the app does not collect the form itself.
  • PcbYearToDate adds the month's claims to ∑LP and zakat to Z, subject to each relief's annual cap after subtracting the year's earlier claims.
  • Apply pcb_exempt_cap_yearly, already on the Payroll Item, in PcbYearToDate: sum this year's finalized amounts for that item, and only the amount above the cap is taxable this month. Travel allowance is seeded with RM6,000. Opening figures' exempt_allowances count toward the cap for mid-year joiners.
  • Form EA Part F and C.P.8D relief fields read from these records.

Rules

  • A claim for a month whose run is finalized is refused; enter it in the next month, which is what LHDN allows.
  • Caps live in code with the year they apply to. A new tax year's TP1 list is a code change, reviewed against the LHDN form, never a tenant setting.

Tests

  • Unit: an employee with RM500 monthly travel allowance pays no PCB on it through December (RM6,000 total), and the whole allowance is taxable from month 13 of a mid-year join that already carried RM6,000 in opening figures. A TP1 medical claim over the cap is trimmed. Zakat claimed on TP1 reduces the month's net MTD, and excess carries forward, matching the spec's Z rule.

F9 CP38 instruction record Partial

Law A CP38 is an LHDN notice ordering the employer to deduct a stated amount over stated months on top of MTD. It has a reference, a total and instalments, and it stops when paid.

What changes

  • Replace the flat salary_structures.cp38_monthly with a payroll_cp38_notices table: employee, LHDN reference, notice date, total amount, monthly instalment, first month, last month, remaining balance, status.
  • Run creation takes the active notice's instalment (or the remaining balance if smaller) and writes it to payslips.cp38, then decrements the balance at finalize. The salary-structure field is migrated into a notice with no reference and an open end, so nothing changes for existing data.

Tests

  • Feature: a RM1,000 notice at RM300 a month produces 300, 300, 300, 100, 0 across five runs; deleting a draft run restores the balance.

F10 Off-cycle runs: bonus and final pay Gap

Law A bonus is "additional remuneration" in the PCB spec, computed with its own formula and reported in the same CP39 month. EA s.20: a leaver is paid on the last day. ITA s.83(5): once CP22A is filed, the employer withholds money owed to the leaver until LHDN clears or 90 days pass.

What changes

  • payroll_runs.kind: monthly (default), bonus, final. One monthly run per period stays the rule; bonus and final runs may share a period.
  • A bonus run pulls only Individual Transactions flagged as bonus for that period and computes PCB as additional remuneration on top of the month's normal remuneration, using the monthly run's figures if it exists or projecting them if it does not. EPF applies; SOCSO and EIS do not (annual bonus is excluded from PERKESO wages).
  • A final run is for one employee, prorates to the last working day (F3), pulls encashed leave and any termination amount entered as Individual Transactions, and marks the employee as paid out. It sets held_for_cp22a when F11 has an open CP22A without clearance, and the bank file omits the payslip until HR releases it.
  • CP39 for the month merges monthly, bonus and final runs into one file, because LHDN wants one submission per employer per month.

Tests

  • Unit: PCB on a RM5,000 bonus in June for an employee on RM4,000 matches a hand-worked additional-remuneration case from the spec's Exhibit method. Feature: a held final payslip is absent from the bank file and present after release; the merged CP39 sums both runs.

F11 Lifecycle notices: CP22, CP22A, CP21, SOCSO Form 2, KWSP registration Gap

Law ITA s.83(2): CP22 within 30 days of a new hire. s.83(3): CP22A at least 30 days before employment ends. s.83(4): CP21 when a leaver is departing Malaysia. PERKESO: Form 2 registers a new employee. KWSP: a new employee is registered on first contribution or by KWSP 3.

What changes

  • New table payroll_notices: employee, type, due date, filed date, LHDN or PERKESO reference, filed by, note.
  • When an employee is created with a joined date, the app opens a CP22 notice due 30 days later and a SOCSO Form 2 reminder. When a last working day is set (F2 or offboarding), it opens a CP22A due 30 days before that date, or immediately if the date is nearer.
  • Each notice has a "Prefilled data" view with the fields the MyTax form asks for (name, NRIC, TIN, address, joined or last day, monthly salary), copyable, and a "Mark filed" button that records date and reference. No PDF generation: LHDN's forms are online only since 2024.
  • Open notices show on the HR dashboard and in F12's calendar. An overdue CP22A sets the hold in F10.

Tests

  • Feature: hiring on 1 March creates a CP22 due 31 March; setting last working day 30 June on 1 May creates a CP22A due 31 May; marking filed clears the dashboard item.

F12 Statutory calendar and submission log Gap

Law The monthly and annual deadlines in section 3, and the 6 and 7 year record duties, which mean "when did we file it and what did we file" must be recoverable.

What changes

  • New table payroll_submissions: run (or year for annual items), agency (EPF, SOCSO/EIS, PCB, HRD Corp, EA, Form E), due date, file downloaded at, marked submitted at, submitted by, agency receipt reference, amount paid.
  • Each download from F6 or F7 writes the "downloaded" stamp. HR marks "submitted" with the portal's receipt number after uploading and paying.
  • Payroll screen gets a "Deadlines" tab: the current and next month's due items with state (not started, file ready, submitted, overdue), plus the annual items from January to April.
  • The existing daily scheduled command sends HR one digest email on the 10th and again on the 14th for any unsubmitted item due on the 15th, and on 20 February and 20 March for the annual forms.

Rules

  • Due dates are computed, never stored as configuration: the 15th of the month after the period, last day of February, 31 March. If the 15th is a weekend the due date stays the 15th; agencies count calendar days.

Tests

  • Feature: finalizing August creates five submissions due 15 September; downloading the CP39 stamps it; the digest on 14 September lists the four not yet submitted.

F13 Complete payslip and publish step Partial

Law Employment Regulations 1957 reg. 9: a pay statement to each employee on or before pay day, with the reg. 5(c) particulars. There is no express rule on electronic statements; s.61(3) EA lets the Director-General approve any form of record, and e-payslips are the general practice.

What changes

  • Payslip PDF gains the missing particulars: rate of pay (monthly basic), days employed in the period (F3), ordinary rate of pay and hourly rate used for overtime, overtime hours and multiplier per group (already stored), unpaid leave days, leave taken in the period (from approved leave requests), date of payment (F5), and the employer's EPF and SOCSO numbers (F1) in the footer.
  • New run step after finalize: "Publish payslips". It stamps payroll_runs.published_at, sends each employee the existing app notification and an email saying their payslip for the month is ready, and only then does the self-service list show it. Today an employee can open a finalized payslip before HR is ready.
  • Employee self-service: a "My payslips" list under My Work with one row per published month and the PDF link, plus the year's EA once issued.

Rules

7. Rules that must not be tidied

These cost real effort to get right in August and each looks like a duplication to a fresh reader.

  • Three wage bases. EPF excludes overtime and travel allowance. SOCSO and EIS exclude annual bonus but include overtime. HRD Corp counts only basic plus fixed allowances. Each Payroll Item carries its own flags; no single "wages" figure exists.
  • Two proration divisors. Calendar days for basic pay and allowances on an incomplete month (s.18A). 26 days and 8 hours for unpaid leave and overtime (s.60I ordinary rate). Unifying them is wrong in law.
  • Overtime is raw hours plus a multiplier. Never store pre-multiplied "equivalent hours"; the payslip must print both.
  • Statutory rates are not tenant-editable. New EPF, PERKESO or LHDN figures are a code change with a new fixture from the official PDF in docs/statutory/. HRD Corp's 1% and the RM1,700 floor join that rule.
  • PCB rounds up to the next 5 sen after truncating to 2 decimals. Everything else rounds half up to the sen.
  • Previous-employer figures stay separate. TP3 amounts are added to year-to-date totals deliberately in the calculator, and shown separately on Form EA.
  • PERKESO tables are data, not a formula. The bands below RM500 do not follow a percentage.

8. Permissions, audit, retention

  • Who can do what. HR and the management tier (manager, director) keep their existing payroll rights. New actions (settings, notices, TP1 entry, publish, mark paid, mark submitted) sit behind the same gate. Employees see only their own published payslips and EA. Super-admin can edit tenant statutory fields but never sees payslips of a tenant it is not a member of, as today.
  • Four-eyes. The existing payroll.four_eyes setting still decides whether finalize needs a separate approver. This spec recommends Unijaya turn it on: HR prepares, a director approves, HR finalizes and publishes.
  • Audit. Every statutory file download, publish, mark paid, pay-date override and notice filing writes an AuditLog entry, following the existing statutory CSV pattern. NRIC stays encrypted at rest and is decrypted only into the agency files.
  • Retention. Finalized runs, payslips, submissions and notices are never hard-deleted. The existing run-deletion rules (draft only) stand. Seven years covers both the Employment Act and the Income Tax Act.
  • Throttling and CSV safety. New POST routes take the existing 30-per-minute throttle. Any CSV output goes through the existing Csv::safeRow guard.

9. Testing

  • Statutory math is proven by fixtures transcribed from the official document, asserted row by row, as the EPF, PERKESO and PCB tests already do. HRD Corp and minimum wage get the same treatment: constant plus effective date plus a test citing the instrument.
  • Upload files are proven by golden files: one small run, byte-compared against a fixture built from the agency's own layout example. A change in the layout is a change in the fixture, reviewed against the new document.
  • Workflow rules (readiness gate, seven-day rule, deduction cap, publish gate, CP22A hold) are feature tests with an HR user, a director and an employee, run against MySQL in CI as today.
  • Parallel month. Before go-live, Unijaya runs one real month in both Worksy and AmanahKu, and HR compares every payslip's EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB and net to the sen. Any difference is a bug in AmanahKu until proven otherwise, because Worksy's figures have been accepted by the agencies.
  • IRBM verification of the PCB implementation is a separate, human submission (the five scenarios in docs/statutory/mtd-testing-question-2026.pdf). F8 and F10 close the last two scenarios that the app cannot yet answer, TP1 claims and additional remuneration.

10. Open questions

None of these blocks Phase 1 from starting. Each needs one answer from Unijaya, and the spec states the assumption it proceeds on.

  1. Is Unijaya registered with HRD Corp today, and at what rate? Assumed yes at 1%. Needed for F7's setting and F1's number.
  2. Which account pays salaries, and through which portal? Maybank2u Biz is assumed since that format exists. Decides whether F14 is needed before go-live.
  3. Does anyone currently have a CP38 notice, TP1 claims, or zakat through salary? Decides whether F8 and F9 move into the go-live month.
  4. Are interns paid through payroll with EPF and SOCSO? Assumed yes. If not, they are excluded in F2 and no calculator change is needed.
  5. Has anyone opted out of SKBBK? The flag exists; HR needs to set it from the signed forms before the first run.
  6. Pay day. Assumed the last calendar day of the month. F5 defaults to it and enforces the seventh-day ceiling either way.
  7. Four-eyes on or off for go-live? Recommended on.
