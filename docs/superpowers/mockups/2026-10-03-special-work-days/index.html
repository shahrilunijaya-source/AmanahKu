Special work days

A company can mark a weekday that only works on certain weeks of the month, with its own hours. Unijaya's first Saturday (09:00 to 13:00) becomes the first rule, replacing the hidden tot_saturday flag.

  • Normal working days keep using the employee's branch, client site or WFH hours, same as today.
  • On a date a rule matches, clock in/out uses the rule's start and end instead. Leaving at 13:00 on a first Saturday is on time, not early.
  • Everything on this page is clickable. The demo date is today, Sat 3 Oct 2026, a first Saturday.

1 · Settings → Work week

The existing Work week card gains a Special days section below the weekly toggles. Selected weekdays switch to ink instead of red, so the one red on this card stays the Save button. A weekday that only works through a rule gets a dashed amber edge.

3 · What changes underneath

Not a screen, but this is what the mockup commits to for the build.

  • New work_day_rules table per company: weekday, which weeks (1st to 4th, last), start, end, half or full. The hidden tot_saturday flag becomes Unijaya's first rule (Sat, 1st, 09:00–13:00, half) and is then dropped.
  • ScheduleResolver checks for a matching rule before falling back to branch, site or WFH hours. The rule's times get stamped on the attendance record at clock in, so the existing late and early checks just work.
  • WorkWeek reads rules instead of the TOT flag, so leave cost, timesheet capacity and reports stay as they are today for the first Saturday.
  • Two rules can't land on the same date. Ticking a weekday as "every week" and also giving it a rule is allowed: the rule's hours win on the weeks it matches.
